# A Healthy Disrespect for AI

## The idea

A standalone essay about keeping curiosity and judgment together when a system performs intelligence convincingly. The existing draft uses staged or symbolic androids, from the Mechanical Turk to present-day AI interfaces, to ask how easily presentation becomes authority. The writer can enjoy the performance while still asking what is actually doing the work and where its limits are.

This piece is independent of the *How to Talk About AI* series, even though its older source project was once associated with that collection.
