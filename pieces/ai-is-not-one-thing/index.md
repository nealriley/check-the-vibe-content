# AI Is Not One Thing

## The idea

Give readers a practical map of the unlike things people label “AI”: research fields, methods, trained models, user-facing services, and workflows built around them. A few concrete examples should let readers identify which layer a claim concerns and what evidence would be relevant to it.

[The Trouble with the Word AI](../the-trouble-with-the-word-ai/index.md) poses the language problem. This piece does the category work that follows. It can be useful on its own as a way to ask, “Which AI are we talking about?”
