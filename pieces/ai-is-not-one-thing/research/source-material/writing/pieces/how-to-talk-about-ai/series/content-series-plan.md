# Content Series Plan

Status: evolving scaffold  
Last updated: 2026-06-13

## Purpose

Use a wider content series to test the book’s framing, build audience, discover reader questions, and create reusable assets for launch.

## Possible formats

- Newsletter or blog essays.
- Short explainers / carousels.
- Talks or workshop modules.
- Cheatsheets and printable guides.
- Short videos.
- Podcast/interview talking points.
- Slide deck based on the book’s concept maps.

## Series pillars

### 1. AI vocabulary without jargon

Potential pieces:

- “AI Is Not One Thing”
- “Model, Tool, Product, Workflow: Four Words That Save AI Conversations”
- “What People Mean by Agents”
- “What Hallucination Is — and Is Not”

### 2. The short history

Potential pieces:

- “A 10-Minute History of AI Hype and Disappointment”
- “From Expert Systems to Chatbots”
- “Why 2017 Matters in Modern AI”

### 3. How the pieces integrate

Potential pieces:

- “LLMs, Search, and Retrieval: Why Your AI Tool Needs Sources”
- “From Prompt to Workflow”
- “When AI Is a Feature, a Product, or a Process Change”
- “From Model to Copilot to Agent”

### 4. How to talk about AI

Potential pieces:

- “The Five AI Conversations People Confuse”
- “How to Talk to an AI Skeptic”
- “How to Talk to an AI Enthusiast”
- “How to Talk About AI Risk Without Starting a Fight”

### 5. What users can do

Potential pieces:

- “A Simple Checklist for Better AI Outputs”
- “Tasks AI Is Good For, Bad For, and Dangerous For”
- “Tasks Are Not Jobs”
- “What AI Changes About Work”
- “Automation vs Augmentation”
- “How to Verify an AI Answer”
- “What Not to Put Into an AI Tool”

### 6. Work, tasks, and workflows

Potential pieces:

- “A Job Is More Than a Task List”
- “What Happens to the Work Around the AI?”
- “Delegating to AI Without Delegating Accountability”
- “Deskilling, Reskilling, and Recombining Work”

## Testing plan

- Pick 3–5 short pieces that represent the book’s promise.
- Publish or privately share them before drafting the full manuscript.
- Track which topics create useful replies, questions, disagreement, or requests for more.
- Feed reader questions back into `book-bible.md`, `weighted-toc.md`, and chapter briefs.

## Reuse rules

- Series pieces can become chapter seeds, but they should be rewritten for book flow.
- Keep dated product claims out of evergreen book chapters unless they are explicitly examples.
- Capture reader language; it may reveal the real target audience.
