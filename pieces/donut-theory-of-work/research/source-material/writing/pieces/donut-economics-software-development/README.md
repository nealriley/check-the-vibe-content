## Framing decision required

**NEAL: What does “Donut” mean in this title?**

- Is it an intentional application or adaptation of Kate Raworth's formal Doughnut Economics framework?
- Or is it a separate metaphor for the gap between cheap code and the full economic, social, and operational cost of software?

The repository contains adjacent material about social needs, ecological and labour harm, commons, and sustainable economic mechanisms, but it contains no primary or secondary source explaining Raworth's framework. Do not imply that connection until it is confirmed and researched.

## Working proposition

AI is lowering the cost of producing code and widening the range of software worth attempting. It does not automatically lower the full cost of useful software: definition, integration, review, security, operation, maintenance, coordination, accountability, skill formation, and resource use remain. The economic question is therefore not simply how much faster code can be generated, but which costs move, which benefits appear, and who receives them.

Treat this as a candidate proposition, not an approved thesis.

## Scope

This piece owns:

- the difference between the cost of code and the total cost of software;
- the new viable scale of software, including personal, temporary, educational, and preservation software;
- the distribution of productivity gains among workers, customers, vendors, and owners;
- persistent or displaced costs such as verification, coordination, maintenance, security, compute, and dependency risk;
- the replenishment of shared inputs such as open-source maintenance, community knowledge, and skill pipelines.

## Boundaries with the other projects

- **The loss of aliveness in the pursuit of productivity** owns the felt experience of participation, meaningful friction, the success that became a non-event, reciprocal celebration, and where saved time goes in lived experience.
- **Everyone is an entrepreneur / everyone is a squad** owns the changing unit of organisation and identity: solo builders, team-like individual capacity, coordination, delegation, and responsibility.
- **Creativity and conformity** owns expressive “thusness,” homogenised output, taste, golden paths versus monoculture, and aesthetic or conceptual sameness.

This piece may cite those tensions, but its centre is economic structure and distribution.
