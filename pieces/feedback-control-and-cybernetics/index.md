# Feedback, Control, and Cybernetics

## The idea

Teach a simple feedback loop through everyday examples: a system senses what happens, compares it with a goal, adjusts, and observes again. Use that loop to explain why cybernetics matters to later conversations about AI, while keeping the fields distinct. Feedback can guide behavior without being model training, and a feedback loop is not automatically AI.

The essay should give readers a useful vocabulary before the series moves into learning and generation.
