# Topic Rollup: Foundations, Wiener, and first-order cybernetics

Foundational works and translations around Norbert Wiener's first-order cybernetics: control, communication, feedback, prediction, and the social questions raised by automation.

## Rollup

This topic currently contains 5 sources spanning 1948–1989. 4 have embedded text sidecars suitable for search and quotation; 1 need OCR or manual inspection before they can support full-text analysis.
