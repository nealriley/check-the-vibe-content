# Topic Rollup: Second-order cybernetics, autopoiesis, and constructivism

Observer-centered and self-referential cybernetics: autopoiesis, radical constructivism, self-organization, cognition, embodiment, and epistemology.

## Rollup

This topic currently contains 11 sources spanning 1974–2012. 9 have embedded text sidecars suitable for search and quotation; 2 need OCR or manual inspection before they can support full-text analysis.

Use this area to track the move from observed systems to observing systems, and from control models to cognition and self-production.
