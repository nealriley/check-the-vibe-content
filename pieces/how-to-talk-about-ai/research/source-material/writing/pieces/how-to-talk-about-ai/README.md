# How to Talk About AI

Project initialized: 2026-06-13

This is a human-authored, AI-assisted nonfiction book project with room for a wider content series.

## Working premise

The book explains the history and language of AI in plain terms, then gives readers a simple way to talk about related topics, understand how they fit together, and use AI tools more effectively without getting trapped by hype, fear, or jargon.

## Editorial posture

This project should stay practical, clear, and reader-trust-first. The book should not become a comprehensive AI textbook, a vendor tutorial, or a hype manifesto.
