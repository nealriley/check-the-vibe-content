# Mechanisms and Organisms

## The idea

A 2026 retelling of a conversation Alan Watts had with IBM engineers, in which he contrasted **mechanisms** (things assembled from parts, understood by taking them apart) with **organisms** (things that grow as a whole, from the inside out). The piece uses the talk as acknowledged inspiration, not as a framework to quote: it describes what computing was like then and what IBM's people were being asked to think about, then carries that conversation forward to the technology available now.

The present-day half should make the argument in its own voice. It should not keep saying "this is what Watts would say". Readers should be able to see the lineage of the ideas without the piece turning into an imagined dialogue with him.
