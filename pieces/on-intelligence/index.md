# On Intelligence: Pattern Engines Grown Under Glass

## The idea

Use the existing “intelligence grown under glass” draft to ask what kind of competence machine systems develop and what the word *intelligence* helps or obscures. Its candidate account of adaptive fit-making draws on patterns, feedback, selection, and inherited human cultural material. The essay should test where that account explains behavior and where it risks making machines sound more like human minds than the evidence supports.

This is the series' conceptual argument about intelligence. [How Machines Learn](../how-machines-learn/index.md) can explain particular training and adaptation processes in more concrete terms.
