# Working brief: Products Become Platforms

## Reader question

When people can remake the software on a product they own, does it start to function as their platform rather than only the manufacturer's finished experience?

## Argument to develop

The television projects reported by The Verge make a larger change visible. Owners are not merely changing a setting; some are replacing a home screen and sharing ways to remove unwanted software. A coding agent can make a device's existing modification routes usable by more people. That changes who can propose an improvement, even when the manufacturer never designed an open extension ecosystem.

The claim needs a boundary. Android developer tools and replaceable launchers were there before the AI-assisted projects. Other TV systems do not offer the same route. A product becomes a platform in this sense only when owners have enough access to inspect, change, and recover it, and when their changes can circulate. The maker can still restrict or reverse that access. The essay should test whether this is a durable shift in control or a temporary opening in one product category.

## Possible shape

1. Open on someone making a TV home screen that serves them instead of the manufacturer's ad and recommendation layout.
2. Explain the existing route: developer mode, Android Debug Bridge, package changes, and alternative launchers. Show what the coding agent adds and what it does not.
3. Move from the modified TV to the product/platform question: who can build on the object, whose priorities appear in its interface, and how improvements are shared.
4. Explore the tension between the owner's preferences and the maker's software, data, and advertising model.
5. Ask which conditions make this repeatable elsewhere, and where closed systems, safety, support, or updates set real limits.

## Reporting and checks

- Find a concrete owner story and inspect an actual change, rather than treating project claims or viral posts as proof of broad adoption.
- Check what each tool changes, what it can restore, and which TV operating systems and models support it. Do not equate disabling named services with ending all data collection.
- Distinguish a user-modifiable product from a vendor-run developer platform with APIs, distribution, and governance.
- Explore who bears the cost when a change breaks a device, a streaming service, accessibility, or a security update.
- Seek a second product category before claiming that this pattern extends beyond TVs.

The [TV case note](research/tv-case.md) records the starting evidence and its limits. This is a brief, not a prose draft.
