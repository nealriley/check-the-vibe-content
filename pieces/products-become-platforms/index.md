# Products Become Platforms

## The idea

A consumer product is usually presented as a finished experience: its maker chooses the interface, features, and business model. But what happens when its owner can ask a coding agent to change the software around it? The starting case is [John Higgins's 23 October 2026 report for The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1016116/vibe-coding-tv-os) on people using coding tools and existing Android TV access to strip back unwanted features and make their own home screens.

The working proposition is that some products can become **platforms for their owners**: surfaces on which people make and share their own improvements. AI may lower the skill and time needed, but it does not create access where the product is closed. The essay should ask what has to be open or reachable for this shift to happen, who controls the result, and how a maker responds when customers remake the experience it intended to sell.
