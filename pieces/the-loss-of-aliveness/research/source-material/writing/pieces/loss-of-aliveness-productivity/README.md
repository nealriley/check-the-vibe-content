# The Loss of Aliveness in the Pursuit of Productivity

Form: exploratory personal essay, with the option to become a practical long-form article
Audience: people adopting AI at work, and leaders deciding what their productivity measures reward

## Reader promise

Use one genuinely enabling AI experience to ask a harder question than whether a tool is useful: when work becomes faster or easier, does the person doing it gain agency, attention, connection, and room to inhabit the result—or does the saved effort disappear into the next task?

The piece should help the reader distinguish needless drudgery from difficulty that carries participation, judgment, learning, or shared meaning. It must not become an argument against AI or against ease.

## Boundary

This project owns the lived consequences of efficiency: participation, agency, reciprocal experience, meaningful versus needless difficulty, and control over the destination of saved time. It does not yet have an approved thesis. The current material supports an ambivalence worth investigating, not a verdict.
