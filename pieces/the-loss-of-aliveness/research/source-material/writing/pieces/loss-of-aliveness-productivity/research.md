## Local evidence pack

### [[../../resources/human-cost-of-digital-transformation/FACTS]]

Potential contextual findings from a local 2026 knowledge-worker research package include:

- F103: 42% said they spend more time verifying AI output than they save.
- F104: 46% said low-quality AI output makes work feel more repetitive or less meaningful.
- F106: 46% said AI output sometimes creates extra work.
- F108: 36% said they often do not understand why they are expected to use AI.
- F097: 65% reported complete autonomy to choose which AI tools they use.
- F073–F075: respondents reported pressure to be more efficient, deliver more, or keep up with AI-assisted colleagues.

The package says its survey covered 2,500 knowledge workers in the UK, US, Canada, Spain, and Germany. It is not developer-specific, and `FACTS.md` explicitly says it does not validate every fact against the underlying survey export. Check the final report, question wording, fieldwork, sample, and public citation before using any figure.

The campaign's “human-centric efficiency” and “remove drudgery without removing meaning” lines (F150/F153) are organisational points of view, not survey findings.

## External leads requiring primary review

- Oliver Burkeman's “Navigating by aliveness”: verify the definition, surrounding argument, and permission/quotation limits before using the phrase as conceptual authority.
- The people and tools in the creative-coding account: confirm spellings, dates, roles, and what each meant to Neal rather than producing a potted history.
- Soundflower and the Mac application: confirm the technical path, language, libraries, and whether Objective-C is the right description.
- *Special Herbs*, Stones Throw, KMD, “Peach Fuzz,” and the instrumental: verify the exact release and relationship.
- Any claim that AI raises quotas, changes staffing, or systematically removes junior learning paths needs direct evidence.

## Missing first-hand evidence

- A traced instance of saved time and where it went.
- A positive AI or automation example that deepened participation.
- One welcome delegation and one protected difficulty.
- A human launch reconstructed with enough detail to isolate what reciprocity contributes.
- The concrete decisions or feedback loops that define “one layer lower.”
- The intended reader and the decision the essay should change.
