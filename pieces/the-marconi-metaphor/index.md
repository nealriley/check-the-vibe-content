# The Marconi Metaphor

## The idea

A standalone essay using radio's spread through everyday life as a way to think about AI becoming a component of many products and activities. The original prose is interested in permeation: a capability can reshape what people build and do without every object becoming the same thing or following one predetermined path.

The historical analogy should help the argument, not stand in for it. Its claims about Marconi, Tesla, radio, and other technologies need checking before they become evidence in a finished piece.
