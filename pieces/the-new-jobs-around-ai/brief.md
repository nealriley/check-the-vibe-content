# Working brief: The New Jobs Around AI

## Reader question

Which human roles does AI deployment create, enlarge, or reshape, and what work do those roles actually do?

## Starting argument to test

Organizations need more than access to a model. Someone has to find a valuable problem, understand the existing work, connect usable context, build the system, set permissions, test it against real outcomes, help people adopt it, and remain answerable when it fails or changes. These responsibilities may produce new titles, but they may also reorganize engineering, product, operations, security, and customer-facing work that already exists.

Atlassian's forward deployed engineer is the opening case. Its description places a senior engineer with a customer team to co-build production agents and workflows. It treats discovery, software, governance, adoption, and business results as one engagement. The essay should inspect what a person in that role does day to day, what remains with the customer's team, and how the work changes after the initial deployment.

## Possible shape

1. Begin with a promising AI pilot that cannot be safely or usefully integrated into real work. Name the missing human labor.
2. Follow a forward deployed engineer from business problem through systems, permissions, code, evaluation, adoption, and handover, using Atlassian as a reported case.
3. Compare the FDE with older field engineering, solutions engineering, consulting, and in-house platform roles. Identify the AI-specific responsibilities without claiming the whole occupation is new.
4. Look for other roles at the interfaces between models and organizations: evaluation, context/data stewardship, workflow ownership, governance, and change management. Verify actual titles and responsibilities before putting them in the essay.
5. Ask who does this work in organizations that cannot buy an embedded vendor team, and what skills early-career people can build toward it.

## Reporting questions

- What does the FDE own during and after an engagement? Who maintains the deployed system and responds when it breaks?
- Which skills are scarce: model knowledge, ordinary software engineering, domain knowledge, organizational trust, or the ability to combine them?
- Are employers adding jobs, renaming jobs, or asking existing workers to absorb new responsibilities?
- How is value measured beyond a shipped agent, and who checks vendor-reported outcomes?
- What is the relationship between FDEs, internal AI teams, product managers, security staff, and frontline workers?
- Does a role designed around senior engineers create a route for junior workers to learn, or narrow that route?

The [source note](research/forward-deployed-engineering.md) records what Atlassian claims and where independent reporting is still needed. This is a brief, not a draft essay.
