# The New Jobs Around AI

## The idea

What kinds of human work become necessary when organizations move from trying AI tools to making them useful in everyday operations? This piece will look at job roles forming or changing around that transition: understanding a real workflow, connecting systems and context, building and evaluating agents, setting authority and safeguards, helping people adopt the result, and maintaining it after launch.

The first case is the **forward deployed engineer**. [Atlassian's October 2026 playbook introduction](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/guides-research/forward-deployed-engineering-playbook) and [enterprise FDE page](https://www.atlassian.com/enterprise/success/fde) describe senior engineers embedded with customers to build production AI in their own systems. The role is a useful lens because it crosses software engineering, domain discovery, workflow design, security, and adoption. Forward deployed engineering existed before generative AI; the question is what the AI deployment problem adds to it.

This is a piece about **people's jobs around AI**, not a catalogue of tasks that AI agents themselves perform. The eventual role list should follow evidence from job descriptions and practitioners rather than assume every new title names a new profession.
