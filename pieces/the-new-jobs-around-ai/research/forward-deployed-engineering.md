# Forward deployed engineering: first case

Research note, 25 October 2026. These are starting sources for the human FDE role, not evidence that its title or responsibilities are universal across AI companies.

## Sources

- [“Atlassian's Forward Deployed Engineering Playbook,” *Inside Atlassian*](https://www.atlassian.com/blog/guides-research/forward-deployed-engineering-playbook), 4 October 2026. Atlassian's account of its engagements and lessons. The article links to a longer playbook; this note relies on the article and product page, not an independent audit of the playbook or its case studies.
- [“Forward Deployed Engineering for Enterprise AI,” Atlassian enterprise page](https://www.atlassian.com/enterprise/success/fde), checked 25 October 2026. The service description, engagement phases, and Atlassian-reported results.
- [Palantir's early-talent role comparison](https://www.palantir.com/careers/students-and-early-talent/), checked 25 October 2026. Describes a forward deployed software engineer focused on one customer's technical and operational outcomes, compared with a product software engineer focused on one capability for many customers.
- [A 2018 interview with Palantir FDE Randall Dorin](https://www.builtinnyc.com/2018/09/24/palantir-meet-your-coworker), *Built In NYC*. Contemporary evidence that people held this role before the generative-AI boom; it does not establish who first created the role.

## What Atlassian says its FDEs do

- Senior engineers work alongside a customer team inside the customer's environment, starting with a business outcome and tracing people, systems, knowledge, handoffs, and permissions before building production code, agents, integrations, and workflow changes.
- The enterprise page describes four phases: **discover, build, adopt, value**. It presents governance, audit trails, and appropriate human oversight as part of design, rather than a check added after the agent works.
- Atlassian says it has worked with more than 100 enterprise customers and deployed more than 80 production agents. Its blog says roughly 12 weeks to self-sufficient teams; its enterprise page says roughly 12 weeks to measurable business value. These are Atlassian's promotional figures and descriptions, not independently verified outcomes or a general industry benchmark.
- The page gives examples in support, manufacturing, and travel. The reported savings and productivity measures should be attributed to Atlassian, with definitions, baselines, and follow-up periods checked before an essay relies on them.

## Editorial use and boundaries

The FDE makes the integration work visible: the engineer is close enough to the customer to understand the workflow and technical enough to change the system. That combination may be especially valuable when an agent requires access to fragmented context and must act within existing permissions. It is a useful example of an AI-era role **adapting an older field-engineering pattern**; calling FDE a job invented by generative AI would overstate the evidence.

The two Atlassian pages show how one vendor sells and describes the role. They do not establish how common FDE hiring is, whether the model outperforms an in-house team, what the job feels like to practitioners, or whether the work remains sustainable after a vendor leaves. Those questions need job-posting comparisons, interviews, and customer-side evidence. Also distinguish a **human FDE job** from products that use “AI FDE” as a name for an automated assistant.
