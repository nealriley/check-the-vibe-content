# The Old Dream of Thinking Machines

## The idea

A selective essay on the older wish to build something that seems to think. Myths, automata, programmable calculation, and tests of machine conversation can illuminate the question without being treated as early versions of today's AI. The piece should separate cultural resemblance from technical lineage.

The reader question is why this dream predates the field that later acquired the name AI. The detailed history after that naming belongs in [When AI Got Its Name](../when-ai-got-its-name/index.md).
