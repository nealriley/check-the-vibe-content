# The Trouble with the Word AI

## The idea

An essay about why conversations go wrong when one word covers unlike methods, systems, products, ambitions, and fears. Follow a familiar disagreement in which people say “AI” but mean different things, then show how naming the subject more precisely changes the conversation.

The question here is what the **word** does to public understanding. [AI Is Not One Thing](../ai-is-not-one-thing/index.md) can give readers the fuller map of categories; this piece should make the need for that map felt.
