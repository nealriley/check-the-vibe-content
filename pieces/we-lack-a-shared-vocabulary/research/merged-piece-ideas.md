# Ideas consolidated into We Lack a Shared Vocabulary

The writer chose to develop the vocabulary and category questions as one essay rather than three separate pieces. The original intake identities remain visible here.

- **CTV-023 — How to Talk About AI:** The opening essay asks why readers need a better map of the field and a reason to care about its distinctions.
- **CTV-024 — The Trouble with the Word AI:** One word can cover unlike systems, products, ambitions, and fears. The shared label shapes public understanding and can obscure responsibility.
- **CTV-028 — AI Is Not One Thing:** Readers need a practical way to distinguish research fields, methods, models, services, and workflows, and to ask what evidence is relevant at each layer.
