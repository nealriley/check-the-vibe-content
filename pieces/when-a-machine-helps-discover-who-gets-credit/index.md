# When a Machine Helps Discover, Who Gets Credit?

## The idea

A standalone piece about attribution when AI helps someone find a possible discovery. Who chose the question, directed the search, recognized a promising lead, checked it, and established that the result is real? Cases from mathematics and archival research could show why finding a candidate and validating a result are different achievements.

The intake idea came from a dated headline collection, not a developed argument. Its claims about particular discoveries, withdrawals, and credit need to be traced to original work and checked before drafting them as facts.
